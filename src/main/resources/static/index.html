About Us

Fuzhou Melbourne Polytechnic (FMP) is a Sino-Australian cooperative institution located in Fuzhou, Fujian Province, China. It is jointly established by Fuzhou University and Box Hill Institute (Australia), offering international-standard vocational and applied education that combines Chinese and Australian teaching practices.

Our Programs

  • Information Technology & Software Engineering
  • Business and International Trade
  • Art and Design
  • English and International Communication

Why Choose FMP

  • Dual-education model blending Chinese and Australian curricula
  • Industry-oriented, hands-on learning approach
  • International pathways for further study abroad
  • Modern campus with advanced teaching facilities
